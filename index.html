About

Farah Jemili holds a PhD in Computer Sciences from the National School of Computer Sciences (ENSI), University of Manouba, and completed her Habilitation to Direct Research (HDR) there in 2024. Since 2011 she has been a Senior Researcher at ISITCom's Mars Research Lab, with earlier research at the Riadi Research Lab on multi-agent negotiation, intrusion detection and prediction, and reasoning under uncertainty. She has authored more than 30 peer-reviewed journal articles and 30+ conference papers, supervised over 25 master's theses and 5 PhD dissertations, and reviewed roughly 200 submissions for journals including Computer Science Review, Artificial Intelligence Review, Ad Hoc Networks, and Computers & Security.
